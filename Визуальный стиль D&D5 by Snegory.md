# Визуальный стиль D&D5 by Snegory

## Основной стиль

- Old 1980s AD&D magazine illustration
- Early *Dragon Magazine* style
- Detailed pen-and-ink engraving
- Gothic medieval fantasy
- Traditional hand-drawn illustration
- Dense cross-hatching and fine linework
- Dramatic chiaroscuro
- Atmospheric fog and deep shadows
- Highly detailed medieval architecture
- Weathered stone, aged wood and corroded metal
- Grim, restrained and historically grounded designs
- Printed vintage illustration look
- Subtle ink imperfections and print grain
- Clean aged-paper background
- Wide, uniform margins
- Solid black single-line frame around the artwork
- Vintage tabletop RPG magazine layout
- No captions
- No page numbers
- No magazine title
- No modern objects
- No photorealism
- No CGI
- No glossy digital painting
- No anime aesthetics

## Атмосфера

- Ancient city beneath eternal fog
- Oppressive gothic mood
- Damp streets and crumbling masonry
- Ravens watching from rooftops and statues
- Narrow alleys, pointed arches and steep roofs
- Abandoned shrines and weathered religious imagery
- Signs of poverty, decay and faded imperial grandeur
- Supernatural menace kept mostly implicit
- The world should appear inhabited, old and exhausted
- Horror through atmosphere rather than excessive gore

## Персонажи

- Historically inspired late-medieval clothing and equipment
- Practical, worn and repaired garments
- Layered wool, linen, leather and mail
- Restrained ornamentation
- Expressive, weathered faces
- Realistic proportions with slight old-school fantasy stylization
- Strong, readable silhouettes
- Natural poses
- Correct anatomy and believable hand placement
- Equipment must look functional and frequently used
- Avoid oversized pauldrons and excessively bulky armour
- Avoid generic high-fantasy costumes
- Avoid modern hairstyles and fashion

## Композиция

- Clear focal point
- Balanced black-and-white values
- Foreground, middle ground and distant background
- Cinematic but restrained staging
- Architecture may dominate the composition
- Figures should feel embedded in the environment
- Use fog to create depth
- Avoid excessive visual noise
- Avoid overcrowding
- Avoid cropped hands, weapons or important architectural elements
- For locations, prefer a distant wide establishing view
- For portraits, use a waist-up or three-quarter composition with a contextual background

## Цветные иллюстрации

When colour is requested:

- Muted vintage print palette
- Desaturated earth tones
- Deep burgundy, faded crimson and dried-blood red
- Moss green, charcoal grey and cold blue-grey
- Tarnished brass and aged parchment
- Limited colour accents
- Matte gouache and acrylic appearance
- Visible brushwork beneath fine ink detailing
- Subtle misregistration and vintage print grain
- No neon colours
- No glossy highlights
- No modern cinematic colour grading
